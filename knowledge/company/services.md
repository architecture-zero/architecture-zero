# Meridian Logistics - services and SLAs

## Ground freight

Scheduled ground freight between the three hubs runs daily. Standard
delivery is 5-7 business days door to door within the service corridor.
Palletized loads only; hazardous materials are not accepted.

## Expedited air freight

Expedited air freight delivers in 2 business days and carries a 40%
surcharge over the ground rate for the same lane. Booking cutoff is 14:00
local time for next-day uplift.

## Damage and loss claims

Damage claims must be filed within 14 days of delivery, with photos and
the delivery receipt. Claims are processed by Finance within 10 business
days of a complete filing. Concealed-damage claims follow the same window,
counted from the delivery date, not the discovery date.

## Contract fulfillment

The Harbor Point warehouse offers pick-pack-ship fulfillment for
e-commerce clients: same-day pick for orders received by noon, inventory
sync every four hours, and quarterly cycle counts. Storage bills per
pallet position per month.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors go through a three-stage review: document intake, compliance
check, and contract signature. Standard turnaround is 10 business days
from a complete document package. Escalations go to
ops@meridian-logistics.test.
