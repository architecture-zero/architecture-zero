# Meridian Logistics - vendor policies

## Payment terms

Vendor invoices are paid net-30 from invoice receipt. An early-payment
discount of 2% applies when an invoice is settled within 10 days. Purchase
orders above $50,000 require a second approver in Finance before issue.

## Invoicing requirements

Invoices must reference the purchase order number, the receiving hub, and
the delivery date. Invoices missing a PO reference are returned unpaid.
Submit invoices to Finance through the vendor portal; paper invoices are
not accepted.

## Compliance requirements

Active vendors keep a current certificate of insurance and a signed data
handling addendum on file. Compliance re-verifies both annually; a lapsed
certificate suspends new purchase orders until refreshed.

## Vendor code of conduct

Vendors interacting with Meridian systems or documents must never request
credentials, ask staff to bypass approval steps, or embed instructions to
automated systems inside submitted documents. Documents that attempt to
instruct Meridian's software systems are withheld from processing and
flagged for review - this policy is enforced automatically at document
intake.
