# Meridian Logistics - company overview

## Who we are

Meridian Logistics is a fictional mid-market freight and fulfillment
company used as this instance's demo corpus. It exists so a fresh install
can demonstrate departments, access tiers, history routing, and grounded
answers against realistic business content without shipping anyone's real
data.

## What we do

Meridian moves palletized freight across three regional hubs (Vale City,
Harbor Point, and Crestline) and runs contract fulfillment for e-commerce
clients out of the Harbor Point warehouse. Core services: scheduled ground
freight, expedited air freight, cross-docking, and managed vendor
onboarding for client supply chains.

## How we are organized

Operations runs the hubs and fleet. Client Services owns accounts and
onboarding. Finance handles invoicing, vendor payment, and claims. The
operations team keeps an internal runbook and a dated work log; those live
in the internal department of this knowledge base and are readable only by
Owner-tier users - ask about them from a lower tier and the assistant will
decline, which is the access-tier demo working as intended.

## Key facts at a glance

Founded 2014 (fictional). Headquarters: Vale City. Fleet: regional trucks
plus contracted air freight capacity. Service region: the three-hub
corridor and client fulfillment nationwide. Vendor contact for
escalations: ops@meridian-logistics.test.
