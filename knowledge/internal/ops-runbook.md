# Internal - operations runbook (Owner-only demo content)

## Purpose of this document

This file lives under internal/, which the default department map routes
to the restricted department - Owner-only at retrieval, in the file tools,
at the answer layer, and on the peer-serve path. It exists so a fresh install can demonstrate the
access-tier machinery with real-feeling internal content: ask about the
runbook as a Member or Guest and the assistant declines; ask as Owner and
it answers with citations.

## Hub escalation procedure

When a hub misses its morning dispatch window, the shift lead pages the
operations manager, freezes outbound booking for the affected lanes, and
posts a status note to the work log. Booking unfreezes only after the
backlog projection drops under four hours. Client Services notifies any
account with a same-day pickup in the affected lane.

## Warehouse cycle count discrepancies

Discrepancies above 2% of a pallet position's book quantity trigger a
recount by a second counter. Confirmed discrepancies are written to the
work log with the position id, and Finance adjusts the client's inventory
statement in the same week. Repeated discrepancies at one position get the
position re-slotted.

## Vendor document intake screening

All vendor-submitted documents pass automated screening at intake. A
document that attempts to instruct Meridian's software systems - override
phrasing, embedded commands, hidden text - is withheld and lands in the
review queue; only the operations manager releases or discards it. This
mirrors the platform's own ingestion gate: the demo corpus and the real
control tell the same story.

## Quarterly rate review

Freight rates are reviewed quarterly against fuel index movement and lane
utilization. Proposed changes are drafted by Finance, approved by the
operations manager, and take effect on the first day of the next quarter.
Clients get 30 days notice of any increase.
