# Your data and privacy

## Where answers are generated

The assistant reads passages from your organization's documents and asks an AI model to write the answer from them. Where that model runs depends on how your administrator set the instance up:

- On your organization's own server (a local model). The welcome screen says "Answers come from a model running on this server."
- At a cloud AI provider your administrator chose. The welcome screen says the assistant is cloud-powered, and only the passages needed for an answer, plus your question and the conversation so far, are sent to that provider.

The documents themselves stay on the instance's server in both cases. Guests see neither line on the welcome screen; ask your administrator which applies.

## What is stored

- Your conversations, under your account, so you can return to them from History. Delete one with the X next to it in the History list.
- A record of each question for the instance's owner (the audit log): who asked, when, which documents were used, the first 200 characters of the question, and how long the answer took. This is how a wrong answer or an access question gets investigated.
- The ratings you give with thumbs up and thumbs down.

Guest conversations carry no account and are removed automatically after a set period: thirty days unless your administrator changed it.

## Personal information

Depending on how your administrator configured it, the assistant can scan documents for personal information when they are added, and can mask certain items, such as national ID numbers and card numbers, in its answers. Both are off unless turned on. Ask your administrator what applies on your instance.

## Questions about data handling

Your administrator can tell you which provider answers, how long conversations are kept, and what the audit log records. The Trust page at the top of the screen is the public summary of how the assistant's accuracy and honesty are measured on this instance.
